Important: This policy describes the current static frontend implementation. It does not claim that server-side accounts, cloud synchronisation, email delivery, analytics, payment processing or other backend services are currently operational.

1 Scope and Responsible Use

This Privacy Policy applies to Gabsweb CashFlow, a personal finance management and productivity application. It should be read together with the Terms and Conditions.

The application is intended to help users organise personal financial and planning information. It is not a bank or a financial institution, and this policy does not describe the handling of bank-held funds or payment accounts.

2 Information Handled

Depending on how you use the application, information may include account names and balances, income and expense entries, transfers, budgets, allocations, savings plans, goals, planned purchases, work schedules, tasks, appointments, projects, milestones, notes, reports, preferences and theme settings.

If you type information into the frontend authentication screens, those screens validate fields locally but do not currently create or authenticate a server account. Do not enter sensitive credentials or information that the current frontend does not require.

3 How Information Is Used

Information entered into the application is used to display the features you request, calculate summaries and progress indicators, save preferences, render dashboards and reports, and support planning activities.

Calculations depend on the information available in the application. The current frontend does not independently verify user entries, and it should not be assumed that information is reviewed by a person.

4 Storage and Retention

The current frontend stores shared application data in browser-based local storage on the device and browser where you use it. The application does not currently claim automatic cloud backup or synchronisation between devices.

Clearing browser storage, deleting website data, changing browsers or devices, or browser failure may remove locally stored information. Keep independent copies of important records. Retention periods, server backups and account deletion arrangements require review when a backend is introduced.

5 Sharing and Third Parties

The current frontend does not represent that it sends your locally stored financial records to a server, sells them, or shares them with third-party data recipients. Do not infer that this remains true after a future backend or integration is introduced.

The application may load third-party libraries or fonts used to display the interface. Those providers may process technical request information under their own privacy policies. Review relevant third-party terms before using the application in an environment where external requests are restricted.

6 Security

Reasonable care should be taken to protect the device and browser used to access the application. Browser-local storage is not a guaranteed security boundary, backup or account protection system.

No absolute security guarantee is made. Do not use the application as the sole repository for information that requires a separately secured or regulated system.

7 Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the role of the responsible operator, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or other protections concerning personal information. The scope and method for exercising those rights must be confirmed for the operating entity and the relevant jurisdiction.

For locally stored information, you may be able to remove it by clearing the relevant browser or website storage. This may be irreversible, so export or preserve important records first where possible.

8 Cookies, Local Storage and Preferences

The current frontend uses browser storage for application data and theme preferences. This is different from claiming that a server account or cloud profile exists. Browser settings may allow you to inspect, restrict or remove this storage, although doing so can affect application behaviour and delete saved records.

9 Children and Sensitive Information

The application has not been assigned a verified age-based service policy in this frontend. Do not use it to submit information about another person without an appropriate lawful basis or permission, and avoid entering highly sensitive information that is not necessary for personal organisation.

10 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application, its storage architecture, integrations or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page and the updated date will be displayed. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate channel where reasonably practicable.

When a Django backend is introduced, this policy must be reviewed and expanded to describe the actual controller or operator, processing purposes, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention, security measures, data-subject processes and complaint routes.

11 Contact and Complaints

Privacy questions, requests and complaints should be directed to the service operator through the contact channel provided for the application.
